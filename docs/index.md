# Learn Zotero in your first week

This academy is for graduate students starting a literature review or thesis. It is built from an analysis of **145 real, public Zotero support questions** — the problems people actually ask for help with, not a feature tour.

{: .note }
> **Unofficial, always check the source.** This is an independent learning project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Zotero or its developers. Every lesson links to the [official Zotero documentation](https://www.zotero.org/support/) and carries a version stamp: *"Last verified against Zotero 10.0.6 documentation on 2026-10-25."*

## How the academy was designed

Every lesson moves an [activation milestone]({{ "strategy/metrics-plan.html" | relative_url }}) — from *first capture* (M1) to *first citation* (M3, the academy's headline activation event) to *sync* (M5). The curriculum comes from the [support question analysis]({{ "about/method.html" | relative_url }}): 62% of the 128 support questions and bug reports fall into five categories, and 40% were knowledge or workflow gaps that education can fix.

## If something breaks

Go straight to the [troubleshooting tool]({{ "troubleshoot/" | relative_url }}). It walks you through the five most common problems from the support data, one fix at a time, and tells you honestly when a problem needs the official forums instead of a fix.
