## What you'll do

By the end you'll have saved your **first three items from the web** into Zotero — records with title, authors, year, and (usually) the PDF. This is **milestone M1**.

## Why it matters

Manual entry is where good intentions go to die. The connector captures metadata and PDFs from journal pages, databases and news sites in one click — and saving *clean* metadata now prevents the citation-formatting problems documented later in this path.

## Steps

1. **Open a paper's page** in your browser — for example, a journal article's abstract page (not the PDF view; the abstract page usually carries the best metadata).
2. **Click the Zotero save button** in the browser toolbar. The icon changes to match what it detected: a journal-article icon on article pages, a book icon on book pages, a folder icon on pages listing several results.
3. **Check the popup.** A small popup confirms which collection the item went to. If you have a collection selected in Zotero, that's where it lands — you can change it in the popup.
4. **Save multiple results at once:** on a search-results page (Google Scholar, a database), the button shows a **folder** icon. Click it, tick the results you want, and save them in one go.
5. **Save a plain web page** when there's no metadata: right-click the save button (in Safari, right-click the page) and choose **Save to Zotero (Web Page with Snapshot)**.
6. **Verify the metadata before moving on.** Click the saved item in Zotero and read the title, authors and year in the right-hand pane. Fix anything wrong *here*, not in your document later.

## Check it worked

The item appears in **My Library** (or your selected collection) with authors and a year filled in, and — on most publisher pages — a **PDF attachment** appears under the item within a few seconds.

## If it didn't work

- Record saves but the **PDF doesn't attach** (common on JSTOR and other databases) → [troubleshooting: Saving fails — the PDF never appears](../../troubleshoot/index.html#flow-connector-unavailable)
- **"Zotero is unavailable"** or nothing happens when you click save → [troubleshooting: Saving fails](../../troubleshoot/index.html#flow-connector-unavailable)
- Items save to the wrong place or metadata looks wrong → re-save from the article's *landing page* rather than the PDF view; see the [official docs on translators](https://www.zotero.org/support/translators) for known site quirks
