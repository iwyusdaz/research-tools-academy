# Organize with collections and tags


## What you'll do

By the end you'll have a **collection with at least 10 items** — **milestone M2** — plus tags you can filter by, and no duplicate items.

## Why it matters

Collections are how you find things in month 3. And duplicates are the quiet tax of saving from multiple databases: the support analysis shows "find and merge duplicates" recurring as a question until it's shown once ([duplicate detection in the official docs](https://www.zotero.org/support/duplicate_detection)).

## Steps

1. **Create a collection:** click **New Collection…** above the left column (or right-click *My Library* → *New Collection…*). Name it after the project — "Thesis chapter 2" beats "misc".
2. **File items:** drag items from the middle column onto the collection. **An item can live in several collections without being copied** — collections behave like playlists, not folders.
3. **Tag by theme or status:** select an item and add tags in the right pane's **Tags** tab, e.g. `to-read`, `methods`, `cite-in-intro`. You can filter by multiple tags in the tag selector at the bottom left.
4. **Color your workflow tags (optional):** right-click a tag in the tag selector → **Assign Color…**. Up to 9 tags get colors and number-key shortcuts (press `1`–`9` with an item selected).
5. **Merge the duplicates:** click **Duplicate Items** in the left column. Zotero pre-selects likely duplicates; check the fields, pick the best version as the master, and click **Merge N Items**. Merging keeps the collections and tags of *both* items; deleting doesn't.

## Check it worked

- Your collection holds **≥ 10 items** (click the collection to count them).
- The **Duplicate Items** view is empty — or you know how to clear it after every big import.

## If it didn't work

- An item "disappeared" from the library after filing — it's still in *My Library*; you added it to a collection, which doesn't move it.
- Zotero offers to merge two items that aren't duplicates — **don't merge**; detection is a suggestion, not a verdict (official docs note you can't mark false positives yet, so just leave them).
- Still stuck → [troubleshooting tool](../../troubleshoot/index.html) or the [official docs on collections and tags](https://www.zotero.org/support/collections_and_tags)
