# Install Zotero and the browser connector


## What you'll do

By the end you'll have the **Zotero desktop app** running and the **Zotero Connector** in your browser — the two pieces that make saving sources possible.

## Why it matters

Zotero is really three pieces: the desktop app (your library), the browser connector (saves what you're looking at), and the word processor plugin (cites into documents, covered in the [Writing with citations path](../writing-with-citations.html)). Most "the save button is missing" problems trace back to one of these pieces never being installed.

## Steps

1. **Download the app.** Go to [zotero.org/download](https://www.zotero.org/download/) and click **Download**. Zotero works on macOS, Windows and Linux.
2. **Install it.** On macOS, open the downloaded `.dmg` and drag **Zotero** into your **Applications** folder, then eject the `.dmg`. On Windows, run the installer.
3. **Open Zotero.** Launch it from your Applications folder, Start menu, or launcher. You do not need an account for steps 1–4 of this path (an account matters for sync — see [lesson 9](../power-user/sync-across-devices.html)).
4. **Install the browser connector.** On the same download page, click **Install Zotero Connector** for your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari) and confirm the browser's prompts.
5. **Pin the button (optional, saves time).** In Chrome or Edge, click the puzzle-piece/extensions icon and pin **Zotero** so the save button is always visible.

## Check it worked

- The Zotero app opens to a window with three columns (left: *My Library*; middle: items; right: details).
- Your browser shows a **Z** button in the toolbar (in Safari: check **Settings → Extensions** and confirm *Zotero Connector* is enabled).

## If it didn't work

- No **Z** button in the browser toolbar → [troubleshooting: The Zotero save button doesn't appear in my browser](../../troubleshoot/index.html#flow-connector-no-button)
- The connector says **"Zotero is unavailable"** when you click save → [troubleshooting: Saving fails](../../troubleshoot/index.html#flow-connector-unavailable)
- The app itself won't open → [official troubleshooting page](https://www.zotero.org/support/kb/), then the [Zotero forums](https://forums.zotero.org/) with a [Report ID](https://www.zotero.org/support/reporting_problems)
