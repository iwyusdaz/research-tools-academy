# Insert citations and choose a style


## What you'll do

By the end you'll have inserted your **first citation into a document** in the citation style your program requires — **milestone M3**, the academy's headline activation event.

## Why it matters

One click captures the citation *with its data*, so the bibliography in lesson 7 builds itself. And because the style is applied at insert time, switching from APA to Chicago later is a settings change — not a rewrite.

## Steps

1. **Set the style first:** in your document, click **Document Preferences** in the Zotero tab and choose the style your program requires (APA 7, Chicago 18, your journal's style — any installed style). Don't see it? **Manage styles…** opens thousands more from the [style repository](https://www.zotero.org/styles).
2. **Place the cursor** where the citation belongs.
3. **Click Add/Edit Citation.** A search dialog appears.
4. **Find the item:** type part of a title, an author's last name, or a year. Select the item — it appears as a **shaded bubble**.
5. **Add a page number** (for direct quotes): type `p. 34` (or just `34`) *inside the search box, before pressing Enter*. Zotero attaches it correctly for your style.
6. **Cite two sources at once:** without pressing Enter, keep typing the next item's author/title — both become bubbles in one citation.
7. **Press Enter.** The formatted citation appears at the cursor.
8. **Narrative citation?** ("As Okafor (2023) argues…") Click the inserted citation bubble, tick **Omit Author**, and type the author into your sentence text.

## Check it worked

The citation appears in the *correct style for your document* — for APA, something like `(Nguyen, 2024)`; for a numbered style, `[1]`. Click it and a bubble opens showing the underlying item.

## If it didn't work

- You see **`ADDIN ZOTERO_ITEM`** codes instead of a citation → [troubleshooting: Citations show as field codes or won't update](../../troubleshoot/index.html#flow-citations-field-codes)
- The citation shows **"Anonymous"** or a wrong year — fix the *item in Zotero* first, then press **Refresh** in the Zotero tab; see [troubleshooting flow 4](../../troubleshoot/index.html#flow-citations-field-codes)
- Style question ("it puts a period where my program forbids one") → [Fix common citation formatting errors](fix-formatting-errors.html)
