# Writing with citations

**Who this is for:** you have sources in Zotero and a document to write. Work laptop with locked-down Word counts — this path covers the permission problem specifically.

**What you'll be able to do at the end:** insert citations in your word processor, switch styles, and generate a bibliography that updates itself.

| # | Lesson | Time | Moves milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | [Install the word processor plugin](install-word-plugin.html) | 5 min | M3 (setup) |
| 6 | [Insert citations and choose a style](insert-citations.html) | 7 min | **M3 — first citation** |
| 7 | [Generate and update your bibliography](generate-bibliography.html) | 6 min | **M4 — correct bibliography** |
| 8 | [Fix common citation formatting errors](fix-formatting-errors.html) | 7 min | M3/M4 (retain) |

**Why this path exists:** "the Zotero tab doesn't appear in Word" and "my citations show as ADDIN codes" are among the most frequent, most *fixable* problems in the [support data]({{ "about/method.html" | relative_url }}). Milestone M3 — first citation — is the academy's primary activation event, so this path is deliberately short and direct. The two [job aids]({{ "job-aids/index.html" | relative_url }}) compress lessons 6–8 onto one page each.
