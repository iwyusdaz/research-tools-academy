## What you'll do

By the end you'll have highlighted a PDF, **extracted all annotations into a single note**, and used that note's citations in a document.

## Why it matters

Reading without capture is re-reading. The built-in reader stores annotations *in Zotero* — linked to the exact page — so your highlights become quotable, citable material instead of a yellow blur in some other app's folder.

## Steps

1. **Open the PDF:** double-click the item in your library — it opens in Zotero's built-in reader.
2. **Highlight:** select text and click a color in the popup (or click the highlighter tool first to "lock" the color for repeated highlights).
3. **Add a thought:** click an annotation and type in its **comment** field — later you'll be glad the argument you had is attached to the quote.
4. **Open the Notes pane:** click **Notes** (top right of the reader), then the **+** and choose **Add Item Note from Annotations**. Zotero creates one note containing every highlight and comment, each with a link back to the page.
5. **Write from the note:** open the note in the left pane, copy a quotation — or click the annotation's citation in the note and drag it into a document with the plugin's Quick Copy. Annotations can also be dragged individually from the reader's sidebar into any note.
6. **Know the one big rule:** annotations live in the Zotero database, not inside the PDF file. To share a PDF with annotations visible in other readers, use **Export a PDF** (right-click the item) or export with annotations embedded.

## Check it worked

Your item has a **note** (visible in the right pane's Notes tab) listing every highlight with page links — and clicking one jumps to the exact spot in the PDF.

## If it didn't work

- Highlighting acts oddly on a scanned PDF — image-based scans can't be text-selected; run OCR first (outside Zotero) or take notes manually.
- Your colleague's annotations in a shared PDF confuse you — that's the group workflow; see [Group libraries](group-libraries.html).
- Reader misbehaves (cursor jumps while typing annotation notes, highlights inconsistent) → these are known product bugs in the support data, not your setup: check the [Zotero forums](https://forums.zotero.org/) before re-installing anything.
