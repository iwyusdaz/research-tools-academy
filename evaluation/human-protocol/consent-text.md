# Consent text (read aloud / shown before the session)

*Prepared 2026-10-25. Verbal consent recorded in the session notes is sufficient; nothing is signed and no identifying data is collected.*

---

Thank you for helping with this study. Before we start, here is what will happen and what will be done with it. You can ask anything now, or at any point.

**What this is.** A 30-minute session where you try two tasks on a learning website about Zotero, thinking out loud while I take notes.

**What I will record.** Handwritten/typed notes only: what you clicked, where you hesitated, and your comments. **No name, no email, no screen recording** unless you separately agree to it (you can agree or refuse below and it does not affect the session). Notes refer to you as "P1"–"P5".

**What happens to the notes.** They are used to fix problems in the learning site. Short quotes may be published in the project repository, attributed to a participant number, never to you. If you say something identifying about yourself, I will not quote it.

**What is asked of you.** Two realistic tasks, thinking aloud. There is no right way and no grade. You can skip anything, ask for a hint, or stop at any point without giving a reason.

**Risks.** Minimal: you use your own machine and your own Zotero library if you have one. I will not ask you to delete or modify anything in your existing library. If a task requires changing a setting, I will undo it afterwards.

**Data protection.** No personal data beyond the notes described above is collected, stored, or transmitted. The website itself collects nothing — it has no analytics.

**Consent — please confirm:**

1. I understand what the session involves. (yes / no)
2. I agree that anonymous session notes and possibly anonymous short quotes may be published. (yes / no)
3. Screen recording: (agree / decline — declining is fine)

If all three are acceptable, we'll begin. You can withdraw at any time — just say so.
